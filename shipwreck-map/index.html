to the map centre

    Where to dig. Coordinates point at the middle of the chunk the wreck starts in. The hull can stretch into neighbouring chunks, and ocean wrecks rest on the sea floor, so bring a boat and look down.

    Beached wrecks generate on beach biomes and sit above water. Their map chest often points to buried treasure nearby.

    How it works. Positions and biomes come from cubiomes, a C recreation of Minecraft's world generation, compiled to run in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

    Bedrock Edition places structures differently, so this chart only matches Java Edition worlds. Text seeds are converted the same way the game does it.
