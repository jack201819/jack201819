to the map centre

    Wrecks and ruins. Coordinates point at the middle of the chunk the structure starts in. Hulls and ruins can stretch into neighbouring chunks and rest on the sea floor, so bring a boat and look down.

    Buried treasure only appears when you zoom in, because there are so many. In Java the coordinates are the exact chest block: stand there and dig straight down through the sand. In Bedrock they are the middle of the chunk, so dig around that spot.

    Beached wrecks generate on beach biomes and sit above water. Their map chest often points to buried treasure nearby.

    How it works. Positions and biomes come from cubiomes, a C recreation of Minecraft's world generation, compiled to run in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

    Java or Bedrock. Pick the edition your world was made in. The two editions share biomes since 1.18 but place structures differently, so the same seed gives different results. Bedrock is supported from 1.18 on. In Java, text seeds are converted the same way the game does it.

    Finding a Bedrock seed. Open the world, go to Settings, then Game, and copy the number under Seed. Paste that number here, not the text you typed when you created the world.
